# Busy buttons: survey (#107)

Survey of every button or key that starts an action on a session or item and doesn't finish synchronously, at `main` 2090347. Request: "when I press a button eg the compact button, it should show disabled until the corresponding action is complete (which in the case of a compaction question would actually be after the question itself is closed)".

Today no button has a busy state. Disabled only ever means "doesn't apply": no session selected (`lifecycle_buttons`, tui.py:4620; `controls`, tui.py:4642), Send with nothing queued (tui.py:4658), Send all with nothing queued (tui.py:4715). The only in-flight guard is Relaunch's "already relaunching" toast (tui.py:5026). The UI repaints from the daemon's session views every second (`tick`, tui.py:3729), and those views carry the whole `sessions` row plus `status`, `liveness` and `compacting` (daemon.py:758), so every store-backed signal below is visible to the TUI.

## Recommended mechanism

1. Derive busy from the store, not from a set of presses: nearly every signal above is already a durable column (`host_command`, `compact_plan`, `*_requested_at`, the relaunch column, `model` vs `applied_model`, the nudge item's status, `shell`), so a restart of the TUI or daemon needs no new persistence. One `busy(s) -> set[button_id]` beside `controls` and `lifecycle_buttons` (tui.py:4620, :4642) feeds `show_button`'s `disabled`.
2. Bridge the gap between press and the first view that shows the store change, and the host-memory-only phase of Compact, with an in-memory `pressed: dict[(sid, button_id), monotonic]` on the app. It clears when the store signal lands or the session dies, is gone, or leaves `live`/`stalled`. It needn't survive a restart, because the store state takes over within a tick.
3. Use a fallback timeout per button, keyed per session, taken from the code's own limits: Interrupt, Model and Shell about 30 s; Compact `COMPACT_TIMEOUT` + 60 s; Relaunch `RELAUNCH_WAIT` + 10 s; Relaunch compacting first `COMPACT_RELAUNCH_WAIT`. On expiry, re-enable and toast "no word from <name> after N s".
4. Park and Forget: keep them enabled with their `parking`/`forgetting` caption, since a re-press is how the person reaches Cancel or Force today. Disabling them would remove that path.
5. Fork needs a store marker or a parent sid on its notices before it can show busy. The cheapest option is `sid=parent` on the start, finish and failure announces, with a 2-minute fallback.
